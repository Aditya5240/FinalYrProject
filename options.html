How it works
  • Learned from what you send. After each reply, the AI compares its draft with what you actually sent and rewrites the list: it keeps what still fits, sharpens it, or replaces the weakest one.
  • Used in every reply. All 5 go into the AI's instructions, together with your past replies to similar messages.
  • You stay in control. Edit any line (it becomes pinned), Pin one to keep it unchanged, or remove one and your next reply fills the slot.
