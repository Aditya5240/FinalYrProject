    Your 5 preferences

    After every reply you send, the AI looks at its draft next to what you actually sent and rewrites these 5: it keeps what still fits, sharpens it, or replaces the weakest one. All 5 go into every prompt. Edit any line (it becomes pinned), pin 📌 one to keep it unchanged, or remove one to let the next reply fill the slot.

      Reply history

      Each row is one snip. "Accepted" = sent as-is, "Edited" = you changed the text, "Refined" = you used a refine button, "Discarded" = closed without using it. "✓ sent" = learned from the exact text you sent after Insert, including edits you made on the page. Deleting a row removes it from learning.

      Learning controls

      Personalise replies
      Use your past replies, style and learned rules when writing. Turn off to get generic replies (useful as a comparison baseline).
      Pause learning
      Stop recording new replies, ratings and edits. Already learned information is still used.
      Update preferences automatically
      After every reply you send, ask the AI to update your 5 preferences from it.
      Semantic search (embeddings)
      Find similar past messages by meaning, using an embedding model on your local server. Without it, keyword matching is used.
